- [ ] **Symbiotic Entity** (Wild Shape use, 10 min) — +28 temp HP, Halo damage goes to 2d6, melee attacks deal +1d6 necrotic

---

**Concentration:** Many spells below say "Concentration." This means you can only maintain one at a time — casting a new Concentration spell ends the previous one. Taking damage forces a CON save (DC = half damage taken, minimum 10) to keep it active. Your CON save is +7.

## Three Turn Types

*Pick one. Don't mix them.*

### Control Turn — *"I shape the battlefield."*

| Action | Bonus Action | Reaction |
|---|---|---|
| **Spike Growth** / **Entangle** / **Blight** / **Wither and Bloom** | Shadow Step (if useful) | Halo of Spores |

No attacks. No Flurry. Just cast and reposition.

### Movement Turn (DEFAULT) — *"I move and harass."*

| Action | Bonus Action | Reaction |
|---|---|---|
| **Attack** (Shillelagh: +10, 1d8+5) | **Shadow Step** | Halo of Spores |

This is your panic-proof turn. If calm and safe: Flurry instead of Shadow Step.

### Survival Turn — *"I stay alive."*

| Action | Bonus Action | Reaction |
|---|---|---|
| Attack once or reposition | **Patient Defense** / **Step of the Wind** | Halo of Spores |

No spells unless Wither and Bloom is needed.

## Decision Tree

When it's your turn, ask yourself:

1️⃣ **Am I shaping the battlefield?** → Yes → Cast a spell (Control Turn)
- Many enemies? → **Spike Growth** (2nd, Concentration, 10 min) — 20ft radius difficult terrain, 2d4 per 5ft moved
- Need lockdown? → **Entangle** (1st, Concentration, 1 min) — 20ft square, STR save or restrained
- One big target? → **Blight** (4th) — 8d8 necrotic, CON save for half
- Ally hurt? → **Wither and Bloom** (2nd) — enemies: 2d6 necrotic, one ally: heals 2d6+5

2️⃣ **Do I want to move or feel stuck?** → Yes → **Shadow Step** (Movement Turn)

3️⃣ **Am I in danger?** → Yes → **Patient Defense / Step of the Wind** (Survival Turn)

4️⃣ **Otherwise** → **Attack once** with Shillelagh (+10, 1d8+5)

That's it.

---

## In Danger?

- **Patient Defense** (1 Focus Point, Bonus Action) — Dodge action as bonus action (disadvantage on all attacks against you)
- **Step of the Wind** (1 Focus Point, Bonus Action) — Disengage + Dash as bonus action, jump distance doubled

---

## Spike Growth Active?

- Use **Thorn Whip** instead of melee attack — +10 to hit, 3d6 piercing, pull target 10ft closer (through the spikes for extra 2d4 per 5ft)

## Reactions Quick Check

1. **Halo of Spores** — enemy moves into or starts its turn within 10ft, CON save DC 18 or 1d6 / 2d6 necrotic. Use this almost every round.
2. **Fungal Infestation** — when a Small/Medium creature dies within 10ft, raise it as a zombie with 1 HP. Only if the situation is obvious and useful.

---

## PANIC BUTTON

> **If you freeze, do this. It is always correct.**
>
> 1. **Shadow Step** to safety or advantage (Bonus Action)
> 2. **Attack once** with Shillelagh (+10, 1d8+5) (Action)
> 3. **Halo of Spores** if enemy is near (Reaction)
>
> **Done. That's a good turn.**

---

## Never Do This

- Never cast a spell AND Flurry of Blows in the same turn
- Never Flurry when unsure — Shadow Step is the default bonus action
- Never Shadow Step + Step of the Wind in the same turn (both bonus actions)
- Never try to "optimize Focus usage" — just Shadow Step
- Never think "should I Stun?" — if you have to ask, don't

If you avoid these, the character plays smoothly.
