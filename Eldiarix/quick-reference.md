## Before Combat

- **Pass without Trace** (2nd, Concentration, 1 hr) — party gets +10 Stealth
- **Symbiotic Entity** (Wild Shape use, 10 min) — +28 temp HP, Halo becomes 2d6, melee +1d6 necrotic

---

## My Action

- **Spike Growth** (2nd, Concentration) — 20ft radius difficult terrain, 2d4 per 5ft moved
- **Entangle** (1st, Concentration) — 20ft square, STR save or restrained
- **Blight** (4th) — single target, 8d8 necrotic, CON half
- **Wither and Bloom** (2nd) — 10ft radius: enemies 2d6 necrotic (CON half), one ally heals 2d6+5
- **Attack** (Shillelagh) — +10, 1d8+5 magical bludgeoning
- **Thorn Whip** (cantrip) — +10, 3d6 piercing, pull 10ft (great with Spike Growth)

---

## My Bonus Action

- **Shadow Step** — teleport 60ft (dim light to dim light), next melee attack has advantage
- **Shillelagh** (cantrip) — weapon uses WIS, 1d8 damage, magical. Lasts 1 min.
- **Flurry of Blows** (1 FP) — two unarmed strikes (+8, 1d8+3 each)
- **Patient Defense** (1 FP) — Dodge (disadvantage on attacks against me)
- **Step of the Wind** (1 FP) — Disengage + Dash, double jump

---

## My Reaction

- **Halo of Spores** — enemy moves into or starts turn within 10ft, CON DC 18, 1d6 necrotic (2d6 with Symbiotic Entity)
- **Fungal Infestation** — creature dies within 10ft, raise as 1 HP zombie

## Wild Shape (Utility Only)

- **Rat** — squeeze into small spaces, scout ahead
- **Spider** — climb walls, squeeze, scout
- **Bat** — fly 30ft, blindsight 60ft, scout in the dark
