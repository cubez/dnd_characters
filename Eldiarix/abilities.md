## Cantrips

### Shillelagh

**Druid Cantrip** | Bonus Action | Touch | 1 minute

**When I use this:** At the start of a fight (or before), I touch my quarterstaff and it becomes magical.

**What this does:** My quarterstaff uses WIS instead of STR for attack and damage rolls. Damage becomes 1d8. It counts as magical.

**If I use this:** Attack is +10, damage is 1d8+5 magical bludgeoning. Lasts 1 minute — basically the whole fight.

**Reminder:** Cast this once at the start. Then just attack with it. No concentration, no slot, just a bonus action.

---

### Thorn Whip

**Druid Cantrip** | Action | 30ft | Instantaneous

**When I use this:** An enemy is within 30ft and I want to pull them toward me — especially into Spike Growth.

**What this does:** A thorny vine lashes out. Spell attack +10, deals 3d6 piercing (scales at level 11). If the target is Large or smaller, I pull it up to 10ft closer to me.

**If I use this:** +10 to hit, 3d6 piercing, pull 10ft. If Spike Growth is active, the pull forces them through the spikes (2d4 per 5ft = 4d4 extra damage for 10ft pull).

**Reminder:** This is optional — use it when Spike Growth is up. Otherwise Shillelagh melee is usually better (higher damage + Symbiotic Entity bonus).

---

### Guidance

**Druid Cantrip** | Action | Touch | Concentration, 1 minute

**When I use this:** Out of combat, when someone is about to make an ability check.

**What this does:** The target adds 1d4 to one ability check of their choice before the spell ends.

**If I use this:** I touch an ally and they get +1d4 on their next check. Costs concentration.

**Reminder:** Great out of combat. Don't use in combat — it costs your action and concentration.

## 1st-Level Spells

### Entangle

**1st-Level Druid** | Action | 90ft | Concentration, 1 minute

**When I cast this:** I need to lock down a group of enemies and I don't have Spike Growth up (or want a cheaper option).

**What this does:** Plants erupt in a 20ft square. Each creature in the area makes a STR save (DC 18). On a fail, they're **restrained**. The area becomes difficult terrain for the duration. Restrained creatures can repeat the save at end of each turn.

**If I cast this:** Multiple enemies can be locked in place. Restrained means they have disadvantage on attacks, and attacks against them have advantage. Costs a 1st-level slot and concentration.

**Reminder:** Can't have this and Spike Growth at the same time (both concentration). Use Entangle for lockdown, Spike Growth for damage.

## 2nd-Level Spells

### Pass without Trace

**2nd-Level Druid** | Action | Self (30ft radius) | Concentration, 1 hour

**When I cast this:** Before we sneak anywhere. Cast it before entering a dungeon, before an ambush, whenever stealth matters.

**What this does:** Each creature I choose within 30ft gets **+10 to Stealth** checks and can't be tracked except by magical means. Leaves no tracks.

**If I cast this:** The entire party becomes nearly undetectable. My Stealth becomes +18. Most party members jump to at least +12. Costs a 2nd-level slot and concentration.

**Reminder:** This is the best pre-combat buff in the game. Cast it whenever stealth is possible. It lasts an hour.

---

### Spike Growth

**2nd-Level Druid** | Action | 150ft | Concentration, 10 minutes

**When I cast this:** Many enemies, or enemies that need to move, or I want area denial.

**What this does:** A 20ft radius area becomes covered in hard spikes. It's difficult terrain. Any creature that **moves** through it takes **2d4 piercing per 5ft** of movement. The spikes are camouflaged.

**If I cast this:** Massive area control. Enemies take damage every time they move through it. Combine with **Thorn Whip** to pull enemies through for bonus 2d4 per 5ft pulled. Lasts 10 minutes — basically the entire combat.

**Reminder:** Creatures can choose not to move, so pair it with forced movement (Thorn Whip). Don't place it where your allies need to walk.

---

### Wither and Bloom

**2nd-Level Druid** | Action | 60ft | Instantaneous

**When I cast this:** An ally is hurt and enemies are nearby. This does damage AND heals at the same time.

**What this does:** A 10ft-radius sphere. Each creature of my choice in the area takes **2d6 necrotic** (CON save for half). One creature of my choice in the area **regains 2d6+5 HP**. A dead plant in the area crumbles to dust.

**If I cast this:** Deal damage to enemies and heal an ally in one action. 2d6 necrotic (CON DC 18 half) to enemies, 2d6+5 healing to one ally. Not concentration.

**Reminder:** Perfect cycle-witch spell — death feeds life. No concentration means you can keep Spike Growth up.

## 4th-Level Spells

### Blight

**4th-Level Druid** | Action | 30ft | Instantaneous

**When I cast this:** One big enemy that needs to take a lot of damage right now.

**What this does:** Necromantic energy washes over the target. **8d8 necrotic damage**, CON save for half. Plant creatures and magical plants have disadvantage on the save and take maximum damage.

**If I cast this:** The biggest single-target nuke I have. 8d8 = average 36 necrotic damage. CON save DC 18 for half. Not concentration.

**Reminder:** Only one 4th-level slot per long rest. Save it for when it matters. Against plant creatures this is devastating (max 64 damage).

## Circle Spells (Available but Not in Core Rotation)

*These are always prepared as Circle of Spores bonus spells. They don't count against your prepared spell limit.*

| Level | Spells |
|---|---|
| 2nd | Blindness/Deafness, Gentle Repose |
| 3rd | Animate Dead, Gaseous Form |
| 4th | Blight (already in rotation above), Confusion |

**When to consider these:** Animate Dead for a durable minion before a dungeon crawl. Blindness/Deafness for a no-concentration debuff. Confusion against groups of enemies. Gaseous Form for infiltration. These are situational — your core rotation is almost always better.

## Druid Features (Circle of Spores)

### Halo of Spores

**Druid Feature** | Reaction | 10ft

**When I use this:** An enemy moves into or starts its turn within 10ft of me.

**What this does:** The creature makes a **CON save (DC 18)**. On a fail, it takes **1d6 necrotic** damage (or **2d6 with Symbiotic Entity** active).

**If I use this:** Free necrotic damage every round on my reaction. No resource cost. Just happens when enemies are near me.

**Reminder:** Use this every single round if an enemy is in range. It's your reaction, and you almost never have a better use for it.

---

### Symbiotic Entity

**Druid Feature** | Action (uses Wild Shape) | 10 minutes

**When I use this:** At the start of combat when I expect a real fight.

**What this does:** I channel Wild Shape into my spores instead of changing form. I gain **28 temporary HP** (4 x Druid level). While temp HP remains: **Halo of Spores** damage increases to **2d6**, and my **melee weapon attacks** deal an extra **1d6 necrotic**.

**If I use this:** +28 temp HP shield, Halo goes from 1d6 to 2d6, melee attacks gain +1d6 necrotic. Uses a Wild Shape charge (2 per long rest). Lasts 10 minutes or until temp HP is gone.

**Reminder:** Activate before combat if possible (it costs an Action). If you used Pass without Trace already, you might activate this on round 1 instead. The temp HP and damage bonuses are both excellent.

---

### Fungal Infestation

**Druid Feature** | Reaction | 10ft

**When I use this:** A Small or Medium creature dies within 10ft of me, and having a zombie ally would be useful.

**What this does:** The dead creature rises as a **zombie** with **1 HP**. It acts on my initiative, obeys my mental commands, and lasts for 1 hour. I can have up to my WIS modifier (5) at once.

**If I use this:** Free zombie minion — can absorb hits, block movement, flank for allies. Uses my reaction (competing with Halo of Spores).

**Reminder:** This uses the same reaction as Halo. Only use it when the zombie would be more valuable than the Halo damage — for example, to block an enemy or provide flanking. Number of uses = proficiency bonus per long rest (5).

## Monk Features

### Focus Points

**Monk Resource** | 6 points | Short Rest recharge

I have 6 Focus Points (the 2024 name for Ki Points), which fuel my monk abilities. They recharge on a short rest.

| Ability | Cost | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Flurry of Blows | 1 FP | Bonus Action |
| Patient Defense | 1 FP | Bonus Action |
| Step of the Wind | 1 FP | Bonus Action |
| Stunning Strike | 1 FP | On hit |

---

### Shadow Step

**Monk Feature (Warrior of Shadow)** | Bonus Action | 60ft

**When I use this:** I'm standing in dim light or darkness and I need to reposition or get advantage on an attack.

**What this does:** I teleport up to 60ft to a spot I can see that's also in dim light or darkness. The first melee attack I make after teleporting has **advantage**.

**If I use this:** Free teleport (no resource cost!) + advantage on next melee attack. I need to be in dim light/darkness and teleport to dim light/darkness.

**Reminder:** This is your go-to bonus action almost every round. No cost. Teleport + advantage. Indoors, dungeons, and nighttime always have dim light somewhere. If the lighting is fully bright, ask the DM about shadows.

---

### Flurry of Blows

**Monk Feature** | 1 Focus Point | Bonus Action

**When I use this:** I want more attacks this round and don't need to Shadow Step.

**What this does:** Immediately after taking the Attack action, I make **two unarmed strikes** as a bonus action.

**If I use this:** Two extra attacks at +8, 1d8+3 each. Costs 1 Focus Point. Good when Shadow Step isn't available or not needed.

**Reminder:** Shadow Step (free + advantage) is usually better than Flurry (costs 1 FP). Use Flurry when you can't Shadow Step or when the extra attacks matter more than advantage.

---

### Patient Defense

**Monk Feature** | 1 Focus Point | Bonus Action

**When I use this:** I'm in danger and need to survive this round.

**What this does:** I take the Dodge action as a bonus action. All attacks against me have **disadvantage** until my next turn.

**If I use this:** Massive defensive boost. Costs 1 FP and my bonus action (so no Shadow Step this round).

**Reminder:** Use when you're taking heavy fire and need to survive. Dodge is incredibly powerful — most attacks will miss.

---

### Step of the Wind

**Monk Feature** | 1 Focus Point | Bonus Action

**When I use this:** I need to escape without provoking or cover a huge distance.

**What this does:** I take the **Disengage and Dash** actions as a bonus action. My jump distance is doubled for the turn.

**If I use this:** I can move 80ft this turn without provoking opportunity attacks. Costs 1 FP.

**Reminder:** Use to escape melee without provoking, or to cover huge distances. Shadow Step is usually better for repositioning if dim light is available.

---

### Stunning Strike

**Monk Feature** | 1 Focus Point | On hit (optional)

**When I use this:** I hit a creature with a melee attack and want to try to stun it.

**What this does:** The target makes a **CON save (DC 18)**. On a fail, it's **stunned** until the end of my next turn. Stunned = can't move, can't take actions, auto-fail STR/DEX saves, attacks against it have advantage.

**If I use this:** If it fails, the target is basically out for a full round. Everyone gets advantage against it. Costs 1 FP.

**Reminder:** Optional and risky — many strong enemies have high CON saves. Best against casters or DEX-based enemies. Don't spam this if targets keep saving.

---

## Wild Shape (Utility Only)

**Druid Feature** | Action | 2 uses per Long Rest

Wild Shape is used for **Symbiotic Entity** (your combat form) or **utility scouting**. Never use it for combat in animal form.

| Form | Speed | Special | Use Case |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rat** | 20ft | Keen Smell | Squeeze through small gaps, scout rooms |
| **Spider** | 20ft climb | Web Sense, Darkvision | Climb walls, scout vertical spaces |
| **Bat** | 30ft fly | Blindsight 60ft, Keen Hearing | Fly ahead in darkness, scout large areas |

**Reminder:** Each use competes with Symbiotic Entity. If combat is likely, save your Wild Shape charges for Symbiotic Entity. Use animal forms only when scouting is clearly needed and combat is unlikely.
