## Who Is Eldiarix?

*A cycle witch who moves through quiet places. She watches, listens, and trusts the natural order. She is not cruel — she simply understands that decay is part of growth, and death is not an ending.*

### Short Intro

> "I move through quiet places, watch the edges, and trust the cycle to sort things out. Nothing truly dies — it just changes hands."

### Long Intro

> I learned stillness from a forest that was burning. I learned patience from fungus breaking stone. I spent years in a hermitage at the edge of a blighted wood, watching the cycle close — how rot fed new growth, how the dead became soil for the living. Most people see death and flinch. I see it and wait, because what comes next is always interesting.
>
> I don't worship nature — I participate in it. My spores are not a weapon, they're a continuation. When I move through shadows, it's not stealth — it's just where I'm most comfortable. I am quiet, watchful, and very patient. And when the cycle needs a push, I push.

## Appearance

- **Build:** Wiry and lean, moves with an unsettling stillness — like someone who's been watching you longer than you realize
- **Face:** Weathered, angular features. Faint fungal discoloration along the jawline and temples
- **Eyes:** Deep-set, steady, rarely blink. The kind of gaze that makes people uncomfortable without understanding why
- **Clothing:** Simple, earth-toned robes and wrappings over monastic garb. Nothing decorative — everything functional
- **Accessory:** A quarterstaff wound with dried moss and faintly luminescent fungal threads
- **Age:** Hard to place. Could be 30 or 50. The hermitage aged her differently
- **Impression:** Looks like a quiet herbalist who wandered in from the woods. Then you notice the spores drifting around her, and the way shadows seem to lean toward her.

## Exploration

**What I'm good at out of combat:**

- **Perception +10 / Passive 25** — I notice everything. Hidden doors, traps, enemies lurking. The DM tells me things before I even ask.
- **Insight +10** — I read people. I know when someone is lying or hiding something.
- **Survival +10** — Tracking, foraging, navigating wilderness, reading weather.
- **Medicine +10** — Stabilize the dying, identify diseases, understand poisons.
- **Stealth +8 (+18 with Pass without Trace)** — I move unseen. With PwT active, the entire party does.
- **Nature +5** — I understand plants, animals, weather, terrain.

**Useful abilities:**

- **Guidance** (cantrip) — +1d4 to any ally's ability check. Offer this constantly.
- **Pass without Trace** — +10 Stealth to the party. Cast it before entering any dangerous area.
- **Wild Shape** — Turn into a rat, spider, or bat to scout. Squeeze through bars, climb walls, fly through dark tunnels.
- **40ft Speed** — I'm fast. I can outrun most things.

## Social Situations

**My approach:** I observe first, speak when it matters. Low CHA (-1) means I'm not charming — but I'm perceptive (Insight +10) and I notice things others miss (Passive Perception 25).

**What I can do:**
- Read the room — Insight +10 tells me who's lying, who's nervous, who's hiding something
- Notice details — Passive Perception 25 catches things nobody else sees
- Support allies — Guidance (+1d4) on their social checks
- Stay quiet and watch — sometimes the best social move is noticing the thing nobody else did

**What I shouldn't do:**
- Take the lead in persuasion or deception (CHA -1)
- Pretend to be charming or talkative

## Problem Situations

| Problem | My Solution |
|---|---|
| We need to sneak past something | Pass without Trace + lead the way |
| We need to scout ahead | Wild Shape (bat in darkness, spider on walls, rat through gaps) |
| Someone is dying | Medicine +10 to stabilize, Wither and Bloom to heal in combat |
| We're lost in the wilderness | Survival +10 to navigate and forage |
| We need information from someone | Insight +10 to read them, Guidance for the talker |
| Something is hidden | Passive Perception 25 — I probably already noticed it |
| Trap or hazard ahead | Perception +10 to spot it, Nature +5 to understand it |

## Core Identity

**Background:** Hermit (reflavored as cycle witch / quiet observer)

**Personality:** Patient, observant, comfortable with silence. Not antisocial — just doesn't waste words.

**Motivation:** Understanding and participating in the natural cycle. Not fighting death, but making sure it serves life.

**Fear:** Waste. Things dying for nothing. Destruction without renewal.

**Bond:** The blighted wood where she learned. The cycle that taught her everything.
