## Class Features

### Sneak Attack (7d6)

**Rogue Feature** | Once per turn | On hit

**When I use this:** Every single attack where I have advantage OR an ally is within 5ft of the target.

**What this does:** I deal an extra **7d6 damage** on one attack per turn. The attack must use a finesse or ranged weapon.

**If I use this:** Average +24.5 damage. This is my primary damage source — every turn, no resource cost.

**Reminder:** Once per TURN, not once per ROUND. I can Sneak Attack on my turn AND on a readied attack during someone else's turn. That's 14d6 total per round.

---

### Cunning Action

**Rogue Feature** | Bonus Action

**When I use this:** Every turn — this is how Rogues use their bonus action.

**What this does:** I can **Dash**, **Disengage**, or **Hide** as a bonus action.

**If I use this:** Hide gives me advantage on next attack (= Sneak Attack). Dash doubles my movement. Disengage lets me leave melee without opportunity attacks.

**Reminder:** Fast Hands often replaces this — using a scroll or item as BA is usually better than just Hiding. But Hide is the fallback.

---

### Fast Hands

**Rogue Feature (Thief)** | Bonus Action

**When I use this:** To activate a scroll, use an object, or use Thieves' Tools as a bonus action instead of an action.

**What this does:** I can use the **Use an Object** action as a bonus action. This includes activating magic items and scrolls.

**If I use this:** True Strike scroll as BA → then Attack with advantage as my action. This is the core of the Brilliant Thief engine.

**Reminder:** This is what makes the Thief subclass special. Other Rogues need their action for scrolls — I use my bonus action and still attack. *Note: Using True Strike scrolls via Fast Hands as a bonus action is a DM-approved table ruling. In 2024 rules, True Strike includes the attack as part of the casting, so check with your DM.*

---

### Use Magic Device

**Rogue Feature (Thief)** | Passive

**When I use this:** Always active.

**What this does:** I **ignore all class, race, and level requirements** on the use of magic items. I also have **4 attunement slots** instead of 3.

**If I use this:** I can use Wizard scrolls, Cleric wands, Paladin swords — anything. No restrictions. And I can attune to one extra item.

**Reminder:** This is why I carry scrolls from every class. There is no spell list I can't access through items.

---

### Reliable Talent

**Rogue Feature** | Passive

**When I use this:** Always active on proficient ability checks.

**What this does:** Whenever I make an ability check that uses a proficiency I have, I treat any d20 roll of 9 or lower as a **10**.

**If I use this:** My minimum Arcana check is **25** (10 + 15). I literally cannot fail to activate a Wizard scroll of 2nd level or lower (DC 13). Even 3rd-level scrolls (DC 15) are auto-successes.

**Reminder:** This applies to ALL proficient checks — Stealth (min 24), Investigation (min 25), Sleight of Hand (min 24), Deception (min 21), Persuasion (min 16), Insight (min 15), Perception (min 15).

---

### Uncanny Dodge

**Rogue Feature** | Reaction

**When I use this:** An attacker I can see hits me with an attack.

**What this does:** I **halve the damage** from the attack.

**If I use this:** A 30-damage hit becomes 15. No resource cost, just my reaction.

**Reminder:** Only works against attacks I can see. Doesn't work against spells that require saves (use Evasion for those). Competes with Shield and Absorb Elements for my reaction.

---

### Evasion

**Rogue Feature** | Passive

**When I use this:** Whenever I make a DEX saving throw against an effect that deals half damage on a success (like Fireball).

**What this does:** On a **success**, I take **zero damage**. On a **fail**, I take **half damage** (instead of full).

**If I use this:** Fireball for 28 damage → I save (DEX +9) → 0 damage. Even if I fail → only 14 damage.

**Reminder:** This is always active. Combined with my +9 DEX save, area damage spells are almost irrelevant to me.

---

### Supreme Sneak

**Rogue Feature** | Passive

**When I use this:** When I move at half speed during my turn.

**What this does:** I have **advantage on Stealth checks** while moving at half speed or less.

**If I use this:** Combined with Cloak of Elvenkind (already advantage) and Reliable Talent (minimum 24), I am effectively undetectable.

**Reminder:** Half speed = 15ft of movement. Worth it when stealth matters more than positioning.

---

### Second-Story Work

**Rogue Feature (Thief)** | Passive

**When I use this:** When climbing or jumping.

**What this does:** Climbing doesn't cost extra movement. My jump distance increases by **4 feet**.

**If I use this:** I can climb a wall for 30ft in one turn (no penalty). Useful for vertical positioning with the shortbow.

**Reminder:** Great for getting to elevated sniper positions.

## Feats

### Lucky

**Origin Feat** | 3 Luck Points per Long Rest

**When I use this:** When I really need a d20 roll to go my way — or need an enemy's roll to go wrong.

**What this does:** I spend 1 Luck Point to roll an extra d20 and choose which one to use. I can use it on my attack rolls, ability checks, saving throws, OR on an attack roll against me.

**If I use this:** Basically advantage or disadvantage on demand, 3 times per long rest.

**Reminder:** Save these for critical moments — a key save, a must-hit attack, or forcing an enemy to miss.

---

### Sharpshooter

**Feat** | Passive

**When I use this:** Always active when using ranged weapons.

**What this does:** Attacking at **long range** doesn't impose disadvantage. I ignore **half cover and three-quarters cover**.

**If I use this:** Shortbow effective at full 320ft range. Enemies behind walls and barriers can't hide from me.

**Reminder:** No damage penalty like the old version. Pure upside — ignore cover and range penalties.

---

### Mage Slayer

**Feat** | Reaction / Passive

**When I use this:** When a caster within 5ft of me casts a spell.

**What this does:** I can make a **reaction attack** against the caster. I also have **advantage on saving throws** against spells cast by creatures within 5ft of me.

**If I use this:** Reaction attack = potential Sneak Attack (if conditions met). Advantage on saves from adjacent casters makes me very hard to affect in melee.

**Reminder:** The reaction attack competes with Uncanny Dodge and Shield. Best against high-value caster targets where the extra SA damage is worth more than defense.

---

### Skill Expert

**Feat** | Passive

**When I use this:** Always active.

**What this does:** Granted Insight proficiency and Deception expertise.

**If I use this:** Insight +5 (was +0), Deception +11 (expertise). Kelvar reads people and lies flawlessly.

**Reminder:** Already factored into skill totals.

## Species Features (High Elf)

### Wizard Cantrip

**High Elf Feature** | Changeable daily | INT-based

**When I use this:** I choose one Wizard cantrip each day after a long rest.

**What this does:** I know one Wizard cantrip, using INT as my spellcasting ability. I can swap it daily.

**Best picks:**
- **Minor Illusion** — create distractions, fake sounds, visual cover
- **Mage Hand** — manipulate objects at 30ft, trigger traps safely
- **Prestidigitation** — utility Swiss army knife
- **Message** — silent communication at 120ft

**Reminder:** This changes daily. Pick based on what you expect to need.

---

### Detect Magic

**High Elf Feature** | 1/Long Rest | No Concentration

**When I use this:** When I want to sense magical auras within 30ft.

**What this does:** For 10 minutes, I sense the presence of magic within 30ft. I can use my action to see the aura and determine the school.

**If I use this:** Spot magical traps, hidden enchantments, glamored items, invisible creatures (if magically invisible).

**Reminder:** The racial version requires **no concentration**. I can have this active alongside anything else.

---

### Misty Step

**High Elf Feature** | 1/Long Rest | Bonus Action | 30ft

**When I use this:** Emergency escape or repositioning.

**What this does:** I teleport up to 30ft to an unoccupied space I can see.

**If I use this:** Instant escape from grapples, restraints, pits, surrounded positions.

**Reminder:** Bonus action, so it competes with Fast Hands. Save for emergencies.

## Familiar

### The Owl (Fey)

**Find Familiar scroll** | 1 hour ritual | Permanent until dropped to 0 HP or dismissed

**Form:** Owl. **Spirit type:** Fey — fitting for a High Elf with Fey Ancestry.

**What makes the owl the right pick:**

| Trait | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Flyby** | No opportunity attack when it leaves melee. The only familiar option with this. It dives in, Helps, and leaves clean. |
| **Fly 60ft** | Crosses the battlefield and back in one turn. |
| **Darkvision 120ft** | Sees twice as far in the dark as I do. |
| **Very low HP** | One hit kills it. Keep it out of area damage. |

**The combo — this is why I cast it:**

> 1. Owl flies adjacent to my target (it rolls its own Initiative and acts on its own turn)
> 2. Owl takes the **Help** action — it must be **within 5ft of the target**
> 3. My next attack roll has **advantage** → **Sneak Attack 7d6**
> 4. Owl flies out with Flyby, no opportunity attack

**What this changes:** advantage every round with no setup. I no longer need to Hide for Sneak Attack, and I no longer need True Strike scrolls. **My bonus action is permanently free** for Fast Hands, Thieves' Tools, or repositioning.

**Other uses:**
- **Scouting** — I take a Magic action to see through its eyes and use its senses. It flies ahead in the dark while I stay safe.
- **Delivering touch spells** — it can carry a touch-range spell to a target for me.
- **Watch** — 120ft darkvision on overwatch during rests.

**Reminder:** It can take any action *except* the Attack action. If it drops to 0 HP it vanishes, and bringing it back costs another hour and 10gp of components — so do not fly it into a fireball to save a round.

## Magic Items

### Ring of Spell Storing

**Wondrous Item** | Attunement | 5 spell levels stored

**When I use this:** Defensive reactions or emergency utility.

**What's loaded:** Shield (1st), Absorb Elements (1st), Invisibility (2nd), Feather Fall (1st) = 5 levels total

**Stored spells:**
- **Shield** — Reaction, +5 AC until the start of your next turn (AC 17 → 22)
- **Absorb Elements** — Reaction, halve incoming elemental damage, +1d6 elemental on next melee hit
- **Invisibility** — Action, invisible until I attack or cast (up to 1 hour)
- **Feather Fall** — Reaction, negate fall damage for up to 5 creatures

**Reminder:** These spells use the original caster's spell save DC / attack bonus, not mine. Reload by having a caster cast into the ring during downtime.

---

### Cloak of Elvenkind

**Wondrous Item** | Attunement

**When I use this:** Always worn — passive benefit.

**What this does:** While the hood is up, I have **advantage on Stealth checks** and others have **disadvantage on Perception checks** to see me.

**If I use this:** Combined with Expertise in Stealth (+14) and Reliable Talent (minimum 24), I'm nearly impossible to detect.

**Reminder:** Hood must be up. No action required — just keep it on.

---

### Dust of Disappearance

**Consumable** | Single use

**When I use this:** Full panic — need to disappear immediately, or set up a devastating ambush.

**What this does:** Toss in the air. All creatures within **10ft become invisible** for **2d4 minutes**. The invisibility **does not break on attacking or casting**.

**If I use this:** I (and nearby allies) become invisible and can attack freely without losing invisibility. This is enormously powerful.

**Reminder:** ONE USE ONLY. Save this for life-or-death moments or a fight-winning ambush. This is not regular invisibility — attacking doesn't break it.

---

### Charlatan's Dice

**Wondrous Item** | Attunement

**When I use this:** When I want to guarantee a specific die result.

**What this does:** I can replicate any die result I've previously seen rolled during the current session.

**Reminder:** Track interesting die results throughout the session.

---

### Shortbow +1

**Weapon** | No attunement

**What this does:** +1 to attack and damage rolls. Has the **Vex** weapon mastery.

**Vex:** When I hit, I have **advantage on my next attack** against the same target before the end of my next turn.

**If I use this:** +10 to hit, 1d6+5 damage, and advantage chains to next attack.

---

### Studded Leather +1

**Armor** | No attunement

**What this does:** AC 12 + DEX modifier + 1 = **AC 17**.

#### Find Familiar — cast this one first

**1st level** | 1 hour ritual | Out of combat only | 10gp components

**When I use this:** During the next long rest. This is not a combat scroll — it is a permanent upgrade to my whole engine.

**What this does:** Summons a familiar. Take an **owl** — it flies, it has Flyby (no opportunity attacks), and it can take the **Help** action next to a target.

**If I use this:** The familiar Helps → I have advantage → **Sneak Attack every single round**, without Hiding and without burning a True Strike scroll. That frees my bonus action permanently for items, Thieves' Tools, or repositioning.

**Reminder:** The familiar is fragile (1 HP). Keep it out of area damage. Ask the DM whether the scroll covers the 10gp material component.

---

#### Blink — my best defensive scroll

**3rd level** | Action | 1 minute | No concentration

**When I use this:** A fight I expect to lose, or when I am the target and my reaction is already spent.

**What this does:** At the end of each of my turns, roll a d6. On a 4–5 I vanish to the Ethereal Plane until the start of my next turn — I cannot be attacked or targeted at all.

**If I use this:** A large share of enemy turns simply do not happen to me. No concentration, so it stacks with everything. *(Check the exact die range in the 2024 PHB — the odds shifted between editions.)*

---

#### Invisibility — a second charge

**2nd level** | Action | Concentration, 1 hour

**When I use this:** When the Ring's charge is already spent, or when I want to save the Ring.

**What this does:** Invisible until I attack or cast. Attacking from unseen gives me **advantage** — that is a guaranteed Sneak Attack on the way out.

**Reminder:** Breaks on attack, unlike Dust of Disappearance. Different tool, same drawer.

---

#### Confusion — my only real crowd control

**4th level** | Action | Concentration, 1 minute | WIS save

**When I use this:** Three or more enemies clustered, or one dangerous melee brute I want taken off the board.

**What this does:** 10ft radius. Creatures that fail a WIS save act randomly — many of them do nothing or wander off. They re-save at the end of each of their turns.

**Reminder:** The save DC comes from the **scroll**, not from me (typically DC 15 at this level — confirm with the DM). This costs me my Action, so no Sneak Attack that turn. Worth it against a crowd, never against one target.

---

#### Protection from Poison — the answer to my worst save

**2nd level** | Action | 1 hour | No concentration

**When I use this:** Before anything involving poison, and before I ever drink something from the giant lands again.

**What this does:** Neutralises one poison in the target and grants **advantage on saving throws against poison** plus **resistance to poison damage** for an hour.

**If I use this:** My CON save is only +2 — my weakest link. This scroll patches exactly that hole for a whole hour.

---

#### Divination — the smartest-person-in-the-room scroll

**4th level** | Action (ritual) | 25gp incense

**When I use this:** Before a heist, when the party is genuinely stuck and the DM's answer would change the plan.

**What this does:** One question about a goal, event, or activity within 7 days. I get a truthful reply — usually cryptic.

**Reminder:** Ask a question about *the best course of action*, not a yes/no. Confirm the incense cost with the DM.

### Priority Scrolls to Carry

- **True Strike** (cantrip) — BA via Fast Hands, advantage on next attack, INT-based
- **Shield** (1st) — backup if Ring is empty
- **Haste** (3rd) — if found, enormous power boost
- **Counterspell** (3rd) — shut down enemy casters
- **Fireball** (3rd) — AoE damage when needed

**Reminder:** Always stock up on scrolls during downtime. They are ammunition for my class features.
