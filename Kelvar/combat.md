## Before Combat

- [ ] **Am I hidden?** Get into a hidden position before initiative if possible
- [ ] **What scrolls do I have?** Check scroll inventory — especially True Strike
- [ ] **Ring of Spell Storing loaded?** Shield, Absorb Elements, Invisibility, Feather Fall
- [ ] **Cloak of Elvenkind on?** Advantage on Stealth, disadvantage to spot me
- [ ] **Dust of Disappearance available?** Save for emergencies

## The Brilliant Thief Engine

*Every turn follows this pattern:*

| Bonus Action | Action | Reaction |
|---|---|---|
| **Fast Hands** (scroll/item/Hide) | **Attack** (Shortbow or Rapier) | **Uncanny Dodge / Shield / Absorb Elements / Readied Attack** |

**The key insight:** My bonus action is for items and scrolls (via Fast Hands). My action is for attacking. My reaction is for defense or a second Sneak Attack.

---

## What's the Situation?

### Pick the row that fits:

| Situation | Bonus Action | Action | Reaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ally within 5ft of target | Hide or use item | Attack → SA (advantage not needed) | Uncanny Dodge / Shield |
| Can Hide for advantage | Hide (Cunning Action) | Attack with advantage → SA | Uncanny Dodge / Shield |
| Have Vex from last turn | Hide or use item | Attack with Vex advantage → SA | Uncanny Dodge / Shield |
| No easy advantage source | True Strike scroll (Fast Hands) | Attack with advantage → SA | Save for defense |
| Want double Sneak Attack | Hide or use item | Ready Attack (trigger: enemy turn) | Readied Attack (second SA) |
| In serious danger | Hide | Dodge or Disengage | Shield (AC 22) or Uncanny Dodge |
| Everything going wrong | Dust of Disappearance / Misty Step | Escape | — |

## Round 1 Opener

*Check for free advantage before spending a scroll:*

> **1. Ally within 5ft of target?** → SA is valid, no setup needed
> - BA: Hide (Cunning Action) → Action: Attack → SA 7d6 → Vex chains to Round 2
>
> **2. Can you Hide?** → BA: Hide for advantage (free)
> - BA: Hide → Action: Attack with advantage → SA 7d6 → Vex chains to Round 2
>
> **3. Neither available?** → BA: True Strike scroll via Fast Hands (last resort)
> - BA: True Strike scroll → Action: Attack with advantage → SA 7d6 → Vex chains to Round 2
>
> *True Strike + Fast Hands as a bonus action is a DM-approved table ruling — not all tables allow this.*
>
> **All three paths end the same way: you have Vex advantage for Round 2.**

---

## Subsequent Rounds

> **The Vex Chain — your standard turn after Round 1:**
>
> 1. **Bonus Action:** Hide (Cunning Action) or use a scroll/item
> 2. **Action:** Attack with Vex advantage → Sneak Attack 7d6
> 3. Vex triggers again → chain continues
>
> **As long as you keep hitting the same target, Vex gives you perpetual advantage.**

---

## How You Get Two Sneak Attacks (No Scroll Required)

*Sneak Attack is once per TURN, not once per ROUND. You can get it on your turn AND on someone else's turn.*

> 1. **Your turn — Action:** Ready an Attack (trigger: "when [enemy] moves" or "when [ally] attacks")
> 2. **Your turn — Bonus Action:** Hide, use item, or anything else
> 3. **Enemy's turn:** Trigger fires → your readied attack → **Sneak Attack 7d6**
> 4. **Your NEXT turn — Action:** Attack normally → **Sneak Attack 7d6 again** (new turn)
>
> **Result:** Two Sneak Attacks across two of your turns.

**Important trade-off:** Readying uses your **Action** AND your **Reaction**. On the turn you Ready, you don't attack. And until your next turn, you **cannot** use Uncanny Dodge or Shield — your reaction is committed to the readied attack.

**Requirements for Readied SA:** You need advantage OR an ally within 5ft of the target when the readied attack triggers.

## Where True Strike Scrolls Come In (Optional Upgrade)

*True Strike scrolls solve one specific problem: "I want to Ready an attack but can't guarantee advantage when it triggers."*

> **Exact sequence:**
> 1. **BA:** True Strike scroll via Fast Hands → advantage on next attack *(table ruling)*
> 2. **Action:** Ready an Attack (trigger: enemy action)
> 3. **Enemy turn:** Trigger fires → attack with advantage → SA 7d6 → Vex queues
> 4. **Your next turn:** Attack with Vex advantage → SA 7d6 again

### When to Use a True Strike Scroll

| Situation | Use a Scroll? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hidden (advantage already) | **No** | You already have advantage |
| Ally within 5ft of target | **No** | SA is valid without advantage |
| Vex active from last hit | **No** | You already have advantage |
| Long range, no allies nearby, can't Hide | **Yes** | No other advantage source |
| Boss fight, need guaranteed hit | **Yes** | Insurance on a critical readied attack |

**Scroll economy:** Carry 5–10 True Strike scrolls. Use 1–2 per adventuring day. Never every round — free advantage sources come first.

## Defense Priority

*When you take damage, pick the best reaction:*

| Threat | Reaction | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Big single hit (20+ damage) | **Shield** (Ring) | AC becomes 22 until the start of your next turn |
| Medium hit (can see attacker) | **Uncanny Dodge** | Halve the damage |
| Elemental damage (fire/cold/etc.) | **Absorb Elements** (Ring) | Halve elemental damage, +1d6 on next melee |
| Multiple small hits | **Shield** (Ring) | AC 22 until the start of your next turn, blocks more total hits |
| Falling | **Feather Fall** (Ring) | Negate fall damage |

**Important:** You only get ONE reaction per round. Choose wisely.

**Don't forget Lucky** (3/Long Rest): Spend a Luck Point to roll an extra d20 and choose which to use — on your attacks, saves, checks, or an enemy's attack against you. Save for critical moments.

## PANIC BUTTON

> **If you freeze, do this. It is always correct.**
>
> 1. **Bonus Action:** Hide (Cunning Action)
> 2. **Action:** Attack (Shortbow +10, 1d6+5 + 7d6 Sneak Attack)
> 3. **Reaction:** Uncanny Dodge if hit
>
> **Done. That's a good turn.**

### Full Panic — Get Out

> 1. **Dust of Disappearance** — 10ft invisibility, doesn't break on attack (if available)
> 2. **Misty Step** — 30ft bonus action teleport (1/Long Rest)
> 3. **Invisibility** from Ring — uses your Action (casting from Ring is a Cast a Spell action, not Use an Object)
> 4. **Cunning Action: Dash/Disengage** — run

## Never Do This

- Never waste your bonus action on an off-hand attack — Fast Hands or Hide is always better
- Never forget Reliable Talent on scroll activation checks — minimum Arcana result is 25
- Never use Sneak Attack on your readied attack if you didn't use it on your turn first — always SA on your own turn first
- Never Shield when Uncanny Dodge would be enough — save Ring charges
- Never forget Vex — if you hit last turn, you have advantage this turn on the same target
- Never attack without Sneak Attack — if you don't have advantage or an adjacent ally, find a way to get it first (Hide, True Strike scroll, etc.)
- Never forget you can use ANY magic item regardless of class restrictions — Use Magic Device
