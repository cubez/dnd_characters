## Who Is Kelvar?

*An elven crime lord who retired from the streets but never stopped stealing. Now he steals from dungeons, vaults, and the laws of magic itself. He doesn't break in — he walks in like he belongs.*

### Short Intro

> "I don't fight fair. I fight finished. By the time you realize what happened, I'm already pouring myself a drink."

### Long Intro

> I've been called a thief, a fraud, a merchant prince of shadows. I've been called worse. The truth is simpler: I'm the smartest person in the room, and I've spent a very long elven lifetime making sure that stays true.
>
> I don't cast spells — I collect them. Scrolls, rings, wands, cloaks — every magic item is just a spell someone else prepared for me. While wizards waste decades in towers memorizing incantations, I study the items they make. I study the locks on the vaults that hold them. And I study the people who guard them.
>
> I don't need magic of my own. I just need yours.

## Appearance

- **Build:** Lean, composed, moves with deliberate economy — never hurried, never wasted motion
- **Face:** Sharp features, dark hair swept back, trimmed goatee, always a faint smirk
- **Eyes:** Elven, perceptive, constantly cataloguing the room
- **Clothing:** Well-tailored dark clothing under a black silk cloak (Cloak of Elvenkind). Looks expensive because it is
- **Accessory:** Silver toothpick with a diamond tip — fidgets with it when thinking
- **Age:** Older for an adventurer, younger for an elf. Confident in a way that only comes from centuries of experience
- **Impression:** Looks like a wealthy merchant who wandered into the wrong tavern. Except he didn't wander — he chose this tavern specifically.

## Exploration

**What I'm good at out of combat:**

- **Investigation +15 / Passive 25** — I notice every detail. Hidden compartments, secret doors, forged documents, magical traps. The DM tells me things before I even ask.
- **Arcana +15** — I understand magic better than most wizards. I identify spells, magic items, arcane wards, and planar phenomena.
- **Stealth +14 (+advantage with Cloak/Supreme Sneak)** — I am functionally invisible when I want to be. Minimum Stealth check: 24.
- **Sleight of Hand +14** — Pickpocket, plant evidence, swap items, palm objects. Minimum check: 24.
- **Deception +11** — I lie with absolute conviction. Minimum check: 21.
- **Persuasion +6** — Competent negotiator, not exceptional.
- **Insight +5** — I read people decently. Minimum check: 15.
- **Perception +5** — I spot things. Passive 15 is average, but Investigation is my real sense.

**Useful abilities:**

- **Detect Magic** (1/Long Rest, no concentration) — Scan for magic auras. Active alongside everything else.
- **Daily Wizard Cantrip** — Minor Illusion for distractions, Mage Hand for remote manipulation, Prestidigitation for utility.
- **Reliable Talent** — I literally cannot roll below 10 on any proficient check. Every lock, every scroll, every bluff has a guaranteed floor.
- **Thieves' Tools** — I can pick any lock as a bonus action (Fast Hands). With Reliable Talent, minimum check is high.

## Social Situations

**My approach:** I enter every room like I own it. I don't threaten — I negotiate. I don't demand — I make people want to give me what I want. I am calm, prepared, and always three steps ahead.

**What I can do:**
- Deceive with near-certainty — Deception +11, minimum 21
- Read the room — Insight +5, Investigation +15 (I notice physical tells, documents, escape routes)
- Negotiate competently — Persuasion +6, backed by confidence and information
- Steal or plant things unnoticed — Sleight of Hand +14, minimum 24
- Identify magical items/effects in play — Arcana +15, Detect Magic

**What I shouldn't do:**
- Get into a shouting match (CHA 12 isn't commanding)
- Pretend to be a simple, honest person (I radiate "I'm smarter than you")
- Threaten violence first — I'm a scalpel, not a hammer

## Problem Situations

| Problem | My Solution |
|---|---|
| Locked door / chest / vault | Thieves' Tools as BA (Fast Hands), Reliable Talent guarantees high roll |
| Need to scout ahead | Supreme Sneak + Cloak of Elvenkind — I am invisible |
| Magical trap or ward | Arcana +15 to identify, Investigation +15 to find the mechanism |
| Need to steal something | Sleight of Hand +14, minimum 24 — they won't notice |
| Someone is lying to us | Insight +5 to read them, Investigation +15 to spot inconsistencies |
| Need information about magic | Arcana +15 — I probably know more than the wizard does |
| Identify a magic item | Arcana +15, Detect Magic (no concentration) |
| Need a distraction | Minor Illusion, Prestidigitation, or Charlatan's Dice |
| Everything has gone wrong | Dust of Disappearance, Misty Step, Invisibility — I vanish |

## Core Identity

**Background:** Criminal / Merchant Prince (reflavored as retired crime lord turned adventurer)

**Personality:** Calm, confident, always the smartest person in the room and aware of it. Not arrogant — just precise. Speaks carefully, moves deliberately, never wastes anything — words, motion, or resources.

**Motivation:** The perfect score. The impossible heist. The item that shouldn't exist. Kelvar is driven by the intellectual challenge, not greed. Gold is nice, but solving the puzzle is better.

**Fear:** Being unprepared. Walking into a room without a plan. Kelvar has a contingency for everything because the one time he didn't, someone died.

**Bond:** The collection. Every magic item he carries has a story, a heist, a close call. They're not just tools — they're trophies.
