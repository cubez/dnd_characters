## My Turn

### Bonus Action (pick one)

- **Fast Hands: Scroll** — activate a scroll as BA (True Strike = free advantage)
- **Fast Hands: Item** — use any magic item as BA
- **Hide** (Cunning Action) — Stealth check for advantage on next attack
- **Dash / Disengage** (Cunning Action) — move or escape
- **Misty Step** (1/Long Rest) — 30ft teleport, emergency only

### Action (pick one)

- **Attack** — Shortbow +10 (1d6+5) or Rapier +9 (1d8+4) + Sneak Attack 7d6
- **Ready an Attack** — trigger on enemy turn for second Sneak Attack
- **Dodge** — all attacks against me have disadvantage

### Reaction (pick one)

- **Shield** (Ring) — +5 AC until start of next turn (AC 22)
- **Uncanny Dodge** — halve one attack's damage
- **Absorb Elements** (Ring) — halve elemental damage
- **Readied Attack** — second Sneak Attack on enemy turn
- **Mage Slayer** — attack adjacent caster who casts a spell
- **Lucky** (3/Long Rest) — roll extra d20, pick which to use
- **Feather Fall** (Ring) — negate fall damage

---

## Round 1 Opener

> **Ally adjacent?** → SA valid, just attack. **Can Hide?** → Hide (BA) for advantage. **Neither?** → True Strike scroll (BA, table ruling). → **Attack → Vex chains to Round 2.**

---

## Standard Turn (Round 2+)

> **Hide or scroll (BA)** → **Attack with Vex advantage (Action)** → **Uncanny Dodge/Shield (Reaction)**

---

## Double Sneak Attack

> **Ready Attack (Action)** on your turn → **Readied SA on enemy turn (7d6)** → **Attack + SA on next turn (7d6)**
>
> *Uses your Action + Reaction. No Uncanny Dodge/Shield until your next turn.*
